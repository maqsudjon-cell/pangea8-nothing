**ZED AVLOD School** is a language school in To'raqo'rg'on, in the Namangan region, running since 2020. It prepares students for English, IELTS and CEFR, for the entrance exams of the Ibrat, Presidential, Ijod and Agency schools, and for university entrance subjects. It is also family, which raised the bar rather than lowering it.

The site went live at [zedavlod.uz](https://zedavlod.uz) on 23 July 2026, in Uzbek, Russian and English.

## The results table

Education sites in the region love round numbers: "98% success rate", "500+ happy students". This one has a table of **thirteen real CEFR certificate scores**, the top one C1 and several B2. Every one of them can be checked against the state certificate database. No percentages, no invented success rate.

A table of thirteen honest results looks smaller than a fake 98%. But anyone can check the thirteen, and anyone could check the 98% too, if they wanted to embarrass the school.

## What stayed out

What I left out matters as much as what I put in:

- No certificate ID numbers.
- No photos of the students.
- One official-looking portrait turned up in the folder of photos I was given. Nobody had confirmed who it was, so it stayed out entirely. Captioning an unidentified person as the founder would have been a lie with a face attached to it.

## Built to be found

A new school in a district town is found through search, so the site got the full treatment from day one: an FAQ section marked up as an FAQ for search engines, structured data for an educational organisation with its real coordinates, language alternates for the three versions, its own cookieless analytics, and Search Console verified with the sitemap submitted on the first day.

See it at [zedavlod.uz](https://zedavlod.uz).
