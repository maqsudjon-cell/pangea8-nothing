CTN Language Centre is an English school in Mirzo Ulug'bek district, Tashkent. It teaches General English from A1 to C1, IELTS, a Kids course for ages 6 to 12, a speaking club and more, in groups of 8 to 12, and the first lesson is a free trial.

The site at [ctnlc.uz](https://ctnlc.uz) went live on 21 July 2026, and it is **one self-contained HTML file**.

## One file, everything in it

That one file holds three languages (Uzbek, Russian and English, with the choice remembered), a light and dark theme, scroll animations, the courses, the teachers, an FAQ, a live map, and a lead form. The form does not send anything to a server: it copies the enquiry and opens Telegram, because that is where the school answers people anyway.

The palette started as cyan and ended as a clean blue on a light hero. I got there by looking hard at how a few AI labs design their sites and deciding that education sites here reach for gradients far too readily.

## Images generated, not drawn

The favicon set and the 1200×630 image that appears when someone shares the link were generated by a small Python script, not drawn by hand or by an image model. When the colour changes, regenerating every icon is one command instead of an afternoon.

## The near-miss

The school's repository already held a listening test and about 40 MB of MP3 files. The normal way I deploy, a force-push, would have deleted the audio. I only knew because I looked first, so the deploy went through GitHub's contents API instead, file by file.

## Honest structured data
