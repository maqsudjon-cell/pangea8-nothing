I weld metal grilles in Tashkent for a living. On 1 August 2026 the workshop got a site of its own: **Payvandchi**, at [payvandchi.maqsudjon.com](https://payvandchi.maqsudjon.com).

It sells four things: window grilles, balcony grilles, stair railings and canopies. Everything is made from 10×10 mm bar, priced from 175,000 so'm per square metre, made in 7 to 10 days and installed for free, up to the 25th floor. The customer looks through the catalogue, says the number of the design they like (each photo has one, like № 077), and we measure, make, paint and install it. The pattern can be changed as much as they like.

## Nothing we cannot deliver

We do not make gates. So the word for gate does not appear anywhere on the site, and every photo that showed a door grille was left out rather than implying a service we cannot deliver. Dropping a photo is cheaper than an awkward phone call.

Every photo is a real job, photographed on site. There is no stock imagery anywhere.

## The engineering was weight

The first hero image was 521 KB. On a mobile connection in Tashkent that is a wait you can see. Re-encoded and resized, it is 173 KB: a third of the bytes and no visible difference.

Adding a photo takes two steps: a line in a spreadsheet file, then one build script that regenerates the gallery. That is what made it possible to add a fresh batch of workshop photos on 23 August without it becoming a project. An English version followed on 5 August.

## Two sites for one workshop

Three days later I built the same workshop a second time, with the same photos, as a completely different site: [Reshotkachi](/work/reshotkachi/). Pricing is done with [KVADRAT](/work/kvadrat/).

See the catalogue at [payvandchi.maqsudjon.com](https://payvandchi.maqsudjon.com).
