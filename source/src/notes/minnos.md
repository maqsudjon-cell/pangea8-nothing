Cats leave little gifts on the doorstep. **Minnos** leaves you English.

[minnos.cc](https://minnos.cc) is a very small site with a cat on it. You give her a pet, and she brings you something she found: an English idiom, and every one of them is about cats. *Curiosity killed the cat. Let the cat out of the bag. Raining cats and dogs.* There are 26 of them to collect.

Each idiom comes with its meaning, and one tap shows the explanation in Uzbek. As you collect them they gather in a list of Minnos's little gifts, and when you have found every one she is very proud of you.

## Why something this small

Most of what I build is a tool with a job. Minnos is the opposite: a cosy, low-pressure way to meet a handful of phrases that native speakers use all the time. Idioms are hard to learn from a list because they make no literal sense. Attached to a cat, a tap and a small reward, they stick.

It is also a reminder to myself that the small web is allowed to be playful. Not every page has to convert anybody.

Meet her at [minnos.cc](https://minnos.cc).
