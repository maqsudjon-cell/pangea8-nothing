On 19 October I bought zitou.uz and moved the Uzbek half of [Zitou](/work/zitou/) onto it. Here is what changed on 18 and 19 October, and why.

## Two addresses, one app

[zitou.uz](https://zitou.uz) is now the Uzbek site: the home page, 7,823 word pages, the dictionary guide, HSK lists, grammar, the mock test, articles and China news. [zitou.app](https://zitou.app) keeps the other nine languages and the app.

The split follows one rule. Anything that holds a sign-in or saved progress stays on zitou.app, because a browser keeps those per address, and moving the app would have split every learner in two. Pages people read moved freely: a 301 sends each old URL to its new home, and zitou.app on its own sends a visitor to their language, or to zitou.uz if that language is Uzbek.

The .uz is on purpose. Google treats a country domain as a signal for that country, and a teacher in Tashkent can say "zitou nuqta uz" in class without spelling anything.

Each address has its own sitemaps, robots file, Search Console and Bing Webmaster properties. [zitou.uz/feed.xml](https://zitou.uz/feed.xml) is one full feed: release notes, articles, guides and the latest news together.

## A class joins with one link

A teacher shows a six-digit code. Students type zitou.uz/ and the code. The Worker checks whether a live lesson or a live game has that code and sends them in.

## Lesson 6, with open voices

The HSK 1 course now has all six lessons: slides for the big screen, 162 words to learn five a day, and a word notebook. Lesson 6 is the telephone, shopping, food and the taxi.

The textbook's own recordings are copyrighted, so I did not use them. The words are spoken by native speakers from [audio-cmn](https://github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn) (CC BY-SA), and the sentences by an open speech model, Kokoro.

## The small things

Questions now go to salom@zitou.uz. The footer on every page is sorted into columns: learn, the HSK 1 course, read, info. The Telegram bot's Uzbek profile and the A4 classroom posters point to zitou.uz.

Two numbers to watch over the next month: how Uzbek searches move in Search Console, and, once the posters are up, how many visits come from their QR codes.
