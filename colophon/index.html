Colophon

Made of type, one accent, and a script.

Background is ink-black with a lime wash in the corner. One accent: electric lime — the weld spark. Surfaces are sharp — radius 2px, 1px rules, a 3px seam down the left edge.

Display face is Instrument Serif — wordmark, headlines, and body. No sans. Domains, dates, and nav are IBM Plex Mono. Fonts are self-hosted.

The mark is geometric: T, O, U as cuts, and a square seal for the g’s of .gg. Favicon, apple-touch icon, and OG lockup are generated in code — SVG committed, PNG/JPEG rasterised by scripts/brand.mjs. No stock photos. No model-drawn people.

Motion is CSS plus a couple of rAF loops: wordmark clip-in, card underline, count-up, log-rule, scroll reveals. prefers-reduced-motion turns all of that into instant states.

Source: github.com/maqsudjon-cell/tou. Canonical: tou.gg. The lab archive remains at maqsudjon.com.
