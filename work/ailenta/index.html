Problem

Uzbek AI news is either a Russian translation with the names left in, or nothing. People who want a wire in their language get a Facebook dump.

Constraint

I cannot sit on a news desk. The thing has to publish when I am on a balcony with a grinder in my hand.

What shipped

29 sources. Hourly LLM summaries. Every item linked to the original. RSS and Telegram. Nobody in the loop after the pipeline is up.

The hard part

Uzbek LLM quality. A summary that invents a source is worse than silence. The link back to the original is the load-bearing wall.
