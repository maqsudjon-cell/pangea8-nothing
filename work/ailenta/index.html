Problem

Uzbek AI news is either a Russian translation with the names left in, or nothing. People who want a wire in their own language get a Facebook dump.

Constraint

I cannot sit on a news desk. It has to publish while I am on a balcony with a grinder in my hand.

What shipped

24 sources. Scheduled LLM summaries. Every item linked to the original. RSS and Telegram. Nobody in the loop once the pipeline is up.

The hard part

Uzbek LLM quality. A summary that invents a source is worse than silence. The link back to the original is the load-bearing wall.
