Open live

Problem

A wedding produces a thousand photos on a hundred cameras. Someone makes a WhatsApp group. The album dies in six threads.

Constraint

No app store. No signup. No database bill. One QR on the table, because that is what a guest will actually scan.

What shipped

Guests drop photos into one album. Export the whole thing as one file. Used at a real wedding the week it shipped.

The hard part

A no-app photo album that does not become a graveyard of accounts. If the guest has to install something, you already lost.
