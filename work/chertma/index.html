Open live ↗ Bot ↗uz-lexicon-skeleton ↗

Problem

Uzbek New Latin (ş ç ö ğ) is a pain on a normal keyboard. People skip the marks. Search breaks. Keyboards pretend the language is ASCII.

Constraint

Has to work in any text field, on a phone, without an account. And it has to sit on a real lexicon, not a cute regex.

What shipped

Type on a normal keyboard, get correct Uzbek Latin. A Telegram bot. A dataset: 4.89M word forms, 23.2M bigrams, a skeleton index for keyboard-indistinguishable forms. CC BY 4.0. It feeds the tool.

The hard part

Keyboard-indistinguishable forms. The skeleton index is the product. The bot is the door.
