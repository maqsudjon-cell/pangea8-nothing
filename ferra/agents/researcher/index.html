The agent’s real task

Find, compare and summarize reliable information. Be concise and note uncertainty.

Try prompts like these

  • Compare three reliable sources and summarize the key differences.
  • Explain what is known, what is uncertain and what I should check next.
  • Compare these three options and give me a clear recommendation.

How to add Researcher

  • Open Ferra on your iPhone.
  • Find Researcher in the starter area.
  • Tap Add. Its task and default model are set up for you.
  • Open the agent to chat or change its model in the picker.

Ferra is available on the App Store. Adding an agent happens inside the signed-in app; the website never changes your account or device.

Make it work for you

Give clear context, choose an image-capable model if you attach photos and review important answers. External email or message proposals still need your approval and final native confirmation.
