Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Maqsudjon Polatov (“Ferra”, “I”, “me”) handles information when you use the Ferra iOS app, its backend service and this website. Ferra is an AI-agent app; it is not a service operated by Apple or OpenRouter.

No advertising trackers. No sale of personal data. No third-party analytics SDK in the app.

Ferra does collect and process the account, content and usage information described below. It is therefore not accurate to describe the app as collecting no data.

1. Information Ferra collects

Account information

When you sign in with Apple, Ferra receives and verifies an Apple identity token. The backend stores your Apple user identifier, an internal account ID and your language preference. Your display name and email address are stored if Apple provides them; this can include an Apple private relay address. Ferra never receives your Apple password.

Agents and conversations

Ferra stores the agent names, tasks, selected model IDs, chat threads, messages, AI responses, tool requests and approval decisions needed to provide the app. A report is linked to the reported AI response and includes the reason and any details you choose to submit.

Your OpenRouter key (BYOK)

If you choose to connect your own OpenRouter API key, it travels over HTTPS to Ferra’s backend, is validated with OpenRouter and is stored encrypted using a server-held encryption key. The plaintext key is not stored on your iPhone and is never returned by the API after you submit it. Ferra’s iOS app stores only its own session token in the device Keychain; the backend stores a cryptographic hash of that session token.

Usage and service information

Ferra records model IDs, request counts, prompt and completion token counts, model-reported cost, free-allowance usage and timestamps. The hosting service may process standard operational logs such as IP address, request time and requested route for security and reliability. Ferra does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. How information is used

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To create agents, maintain chat history and request AI responses.
  • To enforce the daily free-message limit and show your usage.
  • To filter prohibited content and receive, hide and review reported responses.
  • To perform an external email or message action only after your explicit approval.
  • To secure, troubleshoot and improve the service, and to meet legal obligations.

3. AI processing and third parties

Apple provides Sign in with Apple. Apple’s own privacy terms apply to that service.

OpenRouter and its model providers receive the content needed to answer your request. This can include your message, relevant conversation history, your agent’s name and task, and tool instructions. When external tools are enabled, proposed tool arguments can include an intended recipient, subject and content; Ferra does not execute the external action before your approval. Model providers have their own data-retention and training policies. Do not submit sensitive information that you do not want processed by an AI provider.

In free-start mode, requests may use the developer’s OpenRouter account, subject to the server-side daily allowance. In BYOK mode, requests use your own OpenRouter account and its charges and terms apply to you.

If you approve an email action, the recipient, subject and body are sent to Resend and the recipient’s mail provider when that connector is enabled. If you approve a message action, the recipient and message are sent to the configured message-delivery provider. Ferra also uses a hosting provider to operate its backend and database. These providers process information only as needed to deliver their services, subject to their own terms.

Ferra may disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or the public. Ferra does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Your account and app content are kept while your account is active. Sessions expire after 30 days and can be ended sooner by signing out. You can delete individual agents and chats in the app.

To delete your entire account, open Settings → Delete Account in Ferra. This removes your account, agents, chats, messages, pending actions, reports, usage history, sessions and encrypted OpenRouter key from the active Ferra database. You can also request deletion by emailing the address below. Temporary infrastructure logs or backups, where maintained by the hosting provider, may expire separately under that provider’s retention schedule; deleted accounts are not intentionally restored to the active service.

5. Security

Ferra uses HTTPS in production, verifies Apple identity tokens against Apple’s public keys, encrypts stored BYOK credentials and limits API access to the owning account. No system is perfectly secure, so Ferra cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

Ferra is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact me so I can review and delete it where required.

7. International processing

Apple, OpenRouter, model providers, delivery providers and the hosting provider may process information in countries other than yours. Their processing is subject to their own terms and applicable safeguards.

8. Your choices and rights

You choose whether Apple shares your name and email, whether to connect a BYOK key and whether to approve an external action. You can remove your BYOK key, delete content or delete your entire account in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights to request access, correction, export or deletion. Contact me to make a request.

9. Website

The Ferra landing and legal pages do not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The developer’s main tou.gg website may have separate privacy practices; this policy covers the Ferra app, API and pages under tou.gg/ferra.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.
