Questions, answered

A few things worth knowing.

Clear answers about models, cost, privacy and control.

What is Ferra?

Ferra is a personal AI-agent app for iPhone. Each agent has a name, one focused task, its own model and separate chat threads. You can start with a ready-made agent or create your own.

Is Ferra free?

Ferra is free to download and includes a daily free-message allowance. There is no in-app subscription. If you connect your own OpenRouter key, model charges and OpenRouter’s terms apply to your own account.

Which AI models can my agents use?

Ferra loads the live OpenRouter catalog, with hundreds of models from providers including OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta and others. You choose a model separately for each agent. Image attachments require an image-capable model.

Can an agent send an email or message on its own?

No. Ferra asks for your approval first. Apple Mail or Messages then opens with the proposed content, and you make the final decision in that app. Preparing or approving a draft is not treated as proof that it was sent.

What happens to my OpenRouter key?

Your key is sent over HTTPS and stored encrypted on Ferra’s backend. The plaintext key is not stored in the app and is never returned to your iPhone after you submit it. You can remove the stored key in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete Account in Ferra. This deletes your account, agents, chats, image attachments, actions, reports, usage history, sessions and encrypted OpenRouter key from Ferra’s active database.
